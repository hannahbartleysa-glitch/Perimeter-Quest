5 Shape Types across 3 Levels:

~ Level 1 — Rectangles and Squares (P = 2(l+w) and P = 4s)

~ Level 2 — Triangles and Parallelograms (P = a+b+c and P = 2(a+b))

~ Level 3 — Regular Hexagons and all shapes mixed together


Learning features built in:

~ Every shape shows its formula and labelled dimensions

~ The 💡 Hint button walks through the full calculation step by step

~ Wrong answers reveal the correct one immediately with the right value

~ Timed rounds add urgency without punishing thinking time


Game mechanics to keep learners engaged:

~ 3 lives and a countdown timer that speeds up at higher levels

~ Streak bonuses reward consecutive correct answers

~ XP bar and level-up progression unlock harder shapes

~ Badges on the results screen celebrate achievements
